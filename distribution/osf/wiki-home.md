# Turkish EV Charging Intent Dataset

The Turkish EV Charging Intent Dataset is a compact, balanced Turkish-language
benchmark for intent classification and routing in electric-vehicle and
charging assistants. It contains 192 synthetic-editorial queries across eight
intent classes with fixed train, validation, and test splits.

The dataset contains no search logs, customer conversations, or personal data.
It is licensed under CC BY 4.0.

## Responsible use

The corpus is deliberately small and template-based. It must not be used to
infer demand, demographics, or individual behavior, and a strong score is not
evidence of production readiness.
